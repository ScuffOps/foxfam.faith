## Approved Compositions

- **Match & Merge:** cozy reliquary workroom with a dominant raised 4x4 altar
  board, restrained wall shelf, moon window, lantern, and quiet interaction
  space.
- **Find Vezmir:** layered priory cloister with foreground, middle, and
  background hiding bands, a constellation fountain, organic clue niches, and
  a clear central navigation lane.
- **Time Runner:** side-profile clocktower with an oversized clock, Roman
  numeral ledges, clock-hand platform language, gold shards, and an immediate
  left-to-right traversal read.
- **Word Garden:** isometric conservatory with broad quiet seven-petal geometry,
  perimeter planter groups, a Blooming Ink vessel, and letter-safe central
  interaction space.

## Production Boundary

The approved PNG remains a raster asset rather than a true vector export. After
explicit stakeholder render approval, the Match & Merge, Time Runner, and Word
Garden panels were losslessly cropped from the approved source, registered in
`gameArtManifest.js`, and verified in their live staged routes. Their canonical
paths are:

- `/assets/game-hub/match-merge/reliquary-room.png`
- `/assets/game-hub/time-runner/clocktower-route.png`
- `/assets/game-hub/word-garden/conservatory.png`

Find Vezmir is intentionally excluded from that exception. Its navigation
mechanic requires separately authored background, room, and foreground planes
that preserve the approved composition while allowing real depth movement.

Concept-scale Vezmir and runner figures are composition references only. Live
characters must use the approved familiar and sprite systems rather than baked
environment figures.
